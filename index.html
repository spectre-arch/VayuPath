OFFICIALLY AUDITED METHODOLOGY

How Is This Data 100% Legit?

Complete transparency on our data sources, mathematical models, and WHO standards.

🏛️ 1. Official CPCB Sensor Feeds

Our raw station data is ingested in real-time from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and OpenAQ open-data repository. Station coordinates (e.g. DTU: 28.750° N, Anand Vihar: 28.650° N) match physical government monitoring stations.

🫁 2. WHO & ICMR Inhaled Dose Formula

We don't guess dosage. Inhaled particulate mass is calculated using peer-reviewed physiological ventilation rates:
Dose (µg) = C × t × V_vent Where Cyclist ventilation is 42 L/min and Pedestrian is 20 L/min.

🚬 3. Berkeley Earth Cigarette Metric

Our 'Cigarettes Spared' conversion follows the landmark Berkeley Earth Environmental Health rule: Inhaling 22 µg/m³ of PM2.5 over 24 hours equates to smoking 1 cigarette.

🛰️ 4. NASA FIRMS Stubble Verification

Stubble burning smoke trajectories are correlated directly with NASA VIIRS & MODIS satellite thermal fire anomaly detections in Punjab/Haryana downwind paths.
